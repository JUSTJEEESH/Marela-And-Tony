Why book us for this

You are planning the biggest day of your life from another country, and every vendor is a stranger on the internet. Here is what you are actually getting: two musicians who have played Lincoln Center and the Waldorf Astoria, who bring their own sound system, who work off your timeline to the minute, and who will learn your first-dance song whether or not we have ever heard it. We do this in English and in Spanish, which matters more than most couples expect when half the guest list flew in from somewhere else.

The three moments we cover

Ceremony

Processional, the moment itself, recessional — plus a microphone for your officiant and your vows. Guests hear every word.

Cocktail hour

The stretch between the ceremony and dinner is where a wedding either lifts off or stalls. This is exactly what Marcela specialises in.

Dinner

Warm, low, bilingual background sets that let people talk — then lift when the speeches and the first dance arrive.

Questions about weddings

Can you learn our first-dance song?

Yes, and it is included. Send it to us when you book and it will be ready on the day. We have never turned a song down.

Do you provide a microphone for the ceremony and speeches?

Yes — a microphone for your officiant, your vows and your toasts is part of every wedding booking. It is the single most common thing couples forget to arrange, and the one guests notice most when it is missing.

Do you play in Spanish as well as English?

Both, fluently, and we move between them inside a single set. If your guest list is split between countries, this is the detail that makes everyone feel like the wedding was for them too.

What if it rains?

It is the Caribbean; it rains, usually briefly. We move under cover with your planner and keep playing. Our gear is protected and weather never costs you extra.

Is there power on the beach?

Most venues have an outlet within reach. Where there is none, we can run a full ceremony on battery power — just tell us early so we bring the right rig.

How far in advance should we book?

High season on Roatán runs roughly December through April and those Saturdays go first. Six to twelve months out is sensible; if your date is closer than that, ask anyway.
