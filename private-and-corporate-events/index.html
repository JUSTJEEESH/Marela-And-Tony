Why book us for this

Rented a villa in West Bay for the week. Flying a leadership team in for a retreat. Opening a property and needing the room to feel alive the second people walk through the door. This is the work we did for New York agencies — Elan Artists, Hank Lane Music — for years, which means we know the difference between music that fills a room and music that takes it over. We come to you, anywhere on the island.

Questions about private & corporate events

Will you come to our villa?

Anywhere on Roatán. Anything past French Harbour may carry a small travel fee, and we will always tell you that up front rather than at the end.

Can you play on a boat?

Yes. Let us know the size of the vessel and whether there is power, and we will bring the right setup.

Can you keep it at background volume?

That is most of what we do. A corporate reception needs music people can talk over; a birthday at 11pm does not. We read the room and adjust.

Do you work with event planners and DMCs?

Regularly, and we are easy to work with — we hit the timeline, we do not need managing, and we communicate in English or Spanish.
